# SALO — 90-second juror demo

1. Open SALO (3 tabs only). Explain that it runs locally without internet.
2. Tap El Niño, choose Bulalacao. Enter 8,000 L stored and 2,500 L/day demand. Tap **Get my action plan** once. The estimate is 3.2 days (without replenishment), and SALO suggests three review / coordination steps.
3. Complete one action, open Report, show "1 / 3" and share the text report.
4. Tap Home → La Niña, choose Naujan, set rainfall scenario and drainage "Reported blocked." Generate a separate plan; drainage review takes priority.
5. Tap Home, type "El Niño sa Bulalacao. May 8000 liters. Daily demand 2500 liters. Gumawa ng plano." Tap Review. Show the mandatory approval preview; no changes are applied until approval.
6. If an actual Android 12+ phone has an on-device recognition engine, demonstrate voice with network disabled. Otherwise explain the voice limitation rather than pretending it is supported.

**Closing line:** "SALO turns local resource observations into immediate, explainable preparedness steps. It does not pretend to forecast the weather or replace official PAGASA warnings."
